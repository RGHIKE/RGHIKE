title: "Solo Camping on Hump Mountain and a visit to Grassy Ridge Bald"
date: 2026-02-18
---
# Day 1
I started at Yellow Mountain Gap Trailhead and hiked up the Overmountain Trail to the AT. Hopped on the AT and took it all the way to Hump. Saw a bunch of deer towards the summit of Hump. The weather that night wasn't great, so I didn't get much of a sunset, but it was still awesome. Camped at the top of Hump Mountain for the night.

# Day 2
Woke up to moderate rain and pretty strong winds in the morning, and sadly it was still overcast, so not much of a sunrise. Headed down the mountain and dropped my heavy gear at my car at the Yellow Mountain Gap Trailhead. Went back up and took the AT to Grassy Ridge Bald. There were a lot of spots with snow and ice, as well as a lot of melting. Was walking through shin-deep mud in parts, but it all ended up being worth it for a beautiful sunset on Grassy Ridge. Headed back down in the dark with my headlamp. All in all, it was a great trip, and I am looking forward to getting back.
